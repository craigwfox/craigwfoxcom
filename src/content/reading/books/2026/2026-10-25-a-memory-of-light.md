# Thoughts
This book was a roller coaster. Sanderson had no qualms about taking out favorite characters, leaving you questioning who is next. I won't say that every side story is concluded, but you are given endings for most of the big story heavy hitters. I enjoyed the long ride and found myself happy with the ending.

I do find myself wondering if Jordan would have ended this series in 14 books or if he would have gone even longer.

For those who wonder if they should "stick it out" for the whole series, if you find yourself fighting to get through the first few books I'd probably call it quits. There are just too many great fantasy series out there to try. On the other hand if you have made it through the first few books and love it, just keep on going. Yes, the is the "slog", but even that I didn't hate. The only issue with the series I had was trying to back to back read them and burning myself out.

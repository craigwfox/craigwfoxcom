# Thoughts about _Us, In Progress_
I went into this book mostly using it as an option for a reading challenge, not really knowing if I would like it or not. What I found was a great collection of short stories showing the lives of young immigrants. It serves to remind us that we all have many common struggles and to remember to have empathy for our fellow human beings.
